About IIIT Vadodara

The Indian Institute of Information Technology Vadodara (IIITV) is an institute dedicated to education, technology and innovation.

The institute provides students with an environment to develop their technical knowledge, problem-solving skills and creativity.

IIIT Vadodara encourages students to participate in academic, technical, cultural and sports activities. The institute also provides opportunities for students to take part in workshops, seminars, technical events, hackathons and other activities.

About Our Website

Our website is designed to provide students and visitors with information about different events and activities at IIIT Vadodara.

It helps users explore upcoming college events, technical programs, sports activities, seminars, hackathons and other student activities.

The website also provides information about different campus events and gives students an easy way to stay updated about activities taking place at the institute.

The institute is commonly known as IIIT Vadodara.
